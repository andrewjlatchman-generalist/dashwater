Dash Drop

Nine offices have run dry across town. Hand a crate of DASH cans to someone at each one, then sprint over the river to Alex Wright at DASH HQ on Water Street.

  • ↑↓←→Drive. WASD works too. Space to brake.
  • DeliverNine offices, one at a time. Follow the coral line on the map to the next office, then slow down outside its front door to hand over the crate.
  • SprintOnce every office is stocked, follow the marker across the bridge to DASH HQ. It's about 30 seconds away, so make it count.
  • HealthEvery crash damages the van and slows it down. Green crosses patch it up. Hit zero and you've broken down.
  • ScoreA quicker route and a healthier van both score more.

H for the horn, C to swap camera, M to mute, P to pause.

A fan-made game by Andrew Latchman, built as a small taste of what I'd bring to the role. Not an official DASH product.
